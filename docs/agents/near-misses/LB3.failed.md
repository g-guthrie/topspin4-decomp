Trick worth knowing before reading these lines: constants that sit in the shared float pool (lbl_880xxxxx, no `__real@` allowed by fn_check) must be extern floats, and the compiler only schedules their loads like literals when the pointer parameters are `__restrict` (that is what matched lb3_vorbis_mdct butterfly_16 and CELT mix_pitch's final loop); constants are otherwise best copied to locals in the loop pre-header, and loops written in the do-while form the compiler generates.
